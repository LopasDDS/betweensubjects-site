About

What the famous psychology studies actually found.

Between Subjects is a small independent channel publishing short videos on psychology and behavioural science. It takes its name from the experimental design where each participant sees only one condition — you compare between subjects, never within one.

The channel covers the studies everyone has heard of, and the gap between what they found and what they are quoted as finding. There are 17 episodes so far, published to YouTube, TikTok and Facebook. Every one of them has a page here with its sources and its limits.

Reading
